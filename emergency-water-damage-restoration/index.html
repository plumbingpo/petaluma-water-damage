Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Petaluma

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
