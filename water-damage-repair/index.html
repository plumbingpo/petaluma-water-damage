When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Petaluma

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.
  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Following water from above, part of the ceiling droops, has cracks or crumbles when touched.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • A water stain should stay unpainted and unpatched while its cause is still active.
  • Curing time for paint, floor adhesive and caulk should pass before the room sees water or hard use.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Photograph damaged materials before they are removed in case your insurer asks later.
  • Completed-repair photos and the scope document belong in your home records.
